About

Focused on saving teeth and restoring confidence.

Dr. Reegha Marwah is an Endodontist dedicated to saving natural teeth through precise, comfortable, and pain-free treatment. She is experienced in the full range of endodontic procedures, including root canal treatment, re-treatment of failed root canals, management of complex and calcified canals, endodontic surgery, and treatment of dental trauma.

Dr. Marwah works with dental loupes, an operating microscope, and rubber dam isolation as standard, along with the latest endodontic technology. This allows her to see fine details inside the tooth, treat it more accurately, keep the area clean and protected, and give her patients the best chance of a long-lasting result.

She believes in clear communication, explaining every step so patients feel informed and at ease throughout their treatment.
